Questions

  1. Given the definition: “Ecosystem services are the benefits that people obtain from ecosystems,” write a 200-word essay explaining four types of ecosystem services, providing specific examples for each type.
  2. Describe the carbon cycle and explain how human activities have altered the natural carbon cycle.
  3. Describe the water cycle and explain how human activities have modified the natural water cycle.
  4. How does loss of biodiversity concern people?
  5. Discuss the importance of soil to society today. Explain how human activities have altered the physical, chemical, or biological properties of native soil.
